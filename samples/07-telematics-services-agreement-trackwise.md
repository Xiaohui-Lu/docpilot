# Fleet Telematics Services Agreement

This Fleet Telematics Services Agreement is entered into on January 1, 2025 between Northwind Logistics Ltd. (the "Customer") and Trackwise Telematics Inc. (the "Supplier").

1. Services. The Supplier shall provide GPS tracking devices, cellular connectivity and the Trackwise fleet dashboard for up to 150 vehicles.

2. Fees. The Customer shall pay a monthly service fee of USD 3,150.00. Invoices are payable within 15 days of the invoice date.

3. Term. The initial term ends on December 31, 2026. This Agreement will automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives 60 days' written notice of non-renewal.

4. Device Returns. On termination the Customer must return all devices within 30 days or pay a replacement fee of USD 120.00 per device.

5. Data. The Supplier collects vehicle location, speed and driver identification data, which may include personal information of the Customer's drivers. The Supplier may use anonymized data to improve its products.

6. Liability. The Supplier's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid in the six (6) months preceding the claim.

7. Price Changes. The Supplier may increase the fees by up to 5% on each renewal with 45 days' notice.
