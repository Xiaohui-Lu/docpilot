# Independent Consulting Agreement

This Consulting Agreement is entered into on August 18, 2026 between Northwind Logistics Ltd. (the "Client") and Apex Routing Consultants LLP (the "Consultant").

1. Services. The Consultant shall redesign the Client's last-mile routing algorithm and deliver a production-ready implementation, documentation and a two-day training workshop.

2. Fees. The Client shall pay a fixed fee of USD 96,000.00 in three milestones: 30% on signing, 40% on acceptance of the prototype, and 30% on final delivery. Expenses above USD 2,000.00 require prior written approval.

3. Timeline. The prototype shall be delivered by November 30, 2026 and the final implementation by February 27, 2027.

4. Exclusivity. During the term and for 12 months afterwards, the Client shall not engage any other routing or optimization consultant without the Consultant's written consent.

5. Intellectual Property. The Consultant retains ownership of all pre-existing tools and libraries and grants the Client a non-exclusive licence to use them. The Client owns the final deliverables once all fees are paid.

6. Price Adjustment. If the project timeline is extended for reasons outside the Consultant's control, the Consultant may increase the fees for the remaining work at its standard daily rate of USD 1,800.00.

7. Liability. The Consultant's total liability for any claim shall not exceed USD 10,000.00.

8. Termination. The Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience with 30 days' notice, but must pay all fees for milestones in progress.

9. Confidentiality. Each party must keep the other party's confidential information secret during the term and for five years afterwards.
