# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is made on March 3, 2026 between Northwind Logistics Ltd. (the "Company") and Harbor Health Partners LLC (the "Recipient"), each a "Party".

1. Purpose. The Parties wish to evaluate a potential partnership for temperature-controlled delivery of medical supplies (the "Purpose").

2. Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means all non-public business, technical and financial information disclosed by one Party to the other, including pricing, routes, customer lists and software.

3. Obligations. The receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information only for the Purpose and shall protect it with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care.

4. Term. This Agreement remains in effect for two (2) years from the date above, ending on March 2, 2028. Obligations regarding trade secrets shall survive in perpetuity.

5. Return of Materials. Within 15 days of a written request, the receiving Party must return or destroy all Confidential Information.

6. Non-Solicitation. During the term, neither Party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other Party who was involved in the Purpose.

7. Remedies. The Parties agree that a breach may cause irreparable harm and that the disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief.

8. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
