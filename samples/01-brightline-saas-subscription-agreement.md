# Master Subscription Agreement

This Master Subscription Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Northwind Logistics Ltd. (the "Customer") and Brightline Software Inc. (the "Provider").

## 1. Services

1.1 The Provider shall make the Brightline Fleet Analytics platform available to the Customer for up to 250 named users during the Subscription Term.

1.2 The Provider shall maintain an uptime of 99.9% each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance.

## 2. Fees and Payment

2.1 The Customer agrees to pay an annual subscription fee of USD 48,000.00, invoiced annually in advance.

2.2 Invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date. Late payments accrue interest of 1.5% per month on the outstanding balance.

2.3 Upon each renewal, the Provider may increase the fees by up to 7% by giving written notice at least 30 days before the renewal date.

## 3. Term and Renewal

3.1 The initial Subscription Term begins on the Effective Date and ends on January 14, 2027.

3.2 This Agreement will automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives 90 days' prior written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term.

3.3 The Provider may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon 60 days' written notice, in which case it shall refund any prepaid fees for the remaining term.

## 4. Data Protection

4.1 The Provider will process personal data of the Customer's drivers (names, phone numbers and GPS locations) solely to deliver the Services and in accordance with applicable privacy law.

4.2 The Provider shall notify the Customer of any security incident affecting Customer Data within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

## 5. Liability and Indemnification

5.1 Except for breaches of confidentiality, each party's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by the Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

5.2 The Customer shall indemnify the Provider against any third-party claims arising from data uploaded by the Customer to the platform.

## 6. General

6.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario.

6.2 Signed on behalf of Northwind Logistics Ltd. by Dana Whitfield, COO, and on behalf of Brightline Software Inc. by Marcus Lee, VP Sales.
