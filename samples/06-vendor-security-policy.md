# Vendor Security and Data Handling Policy

Policy owner: Information Security, Northwind Logistics Ltd.
Version 2.1, approved on April 7, 2026. Next scheduled review: April 7, 2027.

*Fictional sample document created for the DocPilot demo.*

1. Scope. This policy applies to every vendor that stores, processes or transmits Northwind data, including personal information of employees, drivers and customers.

2. Vendor Assessment. Before onboarding, every vendor must complete a security questionnaire and provide a SOC 2 Type II report or ISO 27001 certificate dated within the last 12 months.

3. Data Processing Agreements. Vendors that process personal data must sign a data processing agreement before receiving any data.

4. Encryption. Vendors must encrypt Northwind data in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256 or equivalent).

5. Access Control. Vendors shall enforce multi-factor authentication for all accounts with access to Northwind systems and review access rights at least quarterly.

6. Incident Notification. Vendors must notify the Northwind security team of any confirmed or suspected security incident within 24 hours.

7. Annual Re-assessment. High-risk vendors must be re-assessed every year. The next re-assessment cycle closes on December 15, 2026.

8. Exceptions. Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the Chief Information Security Officer and must be reviewed every 6 months.
