# Commercial Office Lease Agreement

This Lease is entered into on June 1, 2025 between Kingsway Property Holdings Inc. (the "Landlord") and Northwind Logistics Ltd. (the "Tenant").

1. Premises. The Landlord leases to the Tenant Suite 400, 120 Kingsway Avenue, Ottawa, comprising approximately 6,200 square feet.

2. Term. The lease term is three (3) years, commencing July 1, 2025 and expiring June 30, 2028.

3. Rent. The Tenant shall pay monthly base rent of CAD 18,600.00, due on the first day of each month. Base rent shall increase by 3% on each anniversary of the commencement date.

4. Security Deposit. The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of CAD 37,200.00 upon signing.

5. Operating Costs. In addition to base rent, the Tenant shall pay its proportionate share of operating costs, estimated at CAD 4,100.00 per month and reconciled annually by March 31 of the following year.

6. Renewal Option. The Tenant may renew for one additional term of three years by giving written notice no later than December 31, 2027. If the Tenant does not give notice, the Lease ends on the expiry date.

7. Repairs. The Tenant is responsible for all interior repairs and must keep the HVAC system serviced at least twice a year.

8. Indemnity. The Tenant shall indemnify and hold harmless the Landlord from all claims, losses and damages arising from the Tenant's use of the Premises, and the Tenant's liability under this section shall not be limited.

9. Personal Guarantee. The obligations of the Tenant are personally guaranteed by its President.

10. Insurance Review. The Tenant shall deliver proof of liability insurance of at least CAD 5,000,000 every year by January 31, with the next review due January 31, 2027.
